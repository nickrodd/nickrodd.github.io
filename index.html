Nick Rodd

Divisional Fellow at LBL. Particle theorist looking for dark matter.

The goal of my research is to determine what makes up the dark matter of our Universe. I primarily focus on ultralight wave dark matter and the search for dark matter in astrophysical datasets, an approach known as indirect detection. I also work on effective field theory, statistics, and collider physics.
